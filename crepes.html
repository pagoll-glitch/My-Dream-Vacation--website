🥞 Crêpes

A delicious and famous French food.

📖 History

Crêpes originated in Brittany, a region in France. They became popular throughout France and are now enjoyed all around the world.

🥞 What are Crêpes?

A crêpe is a very thin French pancake made using flour, eggs, milk and butter.

They can be sweet or savory. Sweet crêpes can have chocolate, caramel or fruit. Savory crêpes can contain cheese, vegetables or eggs.

📍 Famous Places to Try

Crêperie Parisienne

📍 18 Quai du Louvre, Paris

⭐ Rating: 4.6/5

Try Nutella crêpes, caramel crêpes or savory buckwheat galettes.

Au P'tit Grec

📍 Rue Mouffetard, Paris

A popular place for crêpes and galettes.

💡 Traveler Tips

Try a sweet crêpe with chocolate or caramel for a classic Paris experience.

If you prefer savory food, try a buckwheat galette.
