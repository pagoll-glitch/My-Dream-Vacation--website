🍽️ Traditional French Food

Discover the delicious food and traditions of France.

📖 History

French cuisine has developed over many centuries. Different regions of France have their own special ingredients, dishes and cooking traditions.

Paris became an important center of French food, with many restaurants, cafés and bakeries.

🍴 What is Traditional French Food?

Traditional French food includes many different dishes made using ingredients such as vegetables, cheese, bread, herbs, butter and sauces.

Some famous French foods include baguettes, quiche, ratatouille, French onion soup and different types of cheese.
