🥐 The Croissant

A buttery symbol of French bakery culture

📖 History

The croissant is one of the most recognizable symbols of French baking. Its modern form developed in France from earlier crescent-shaped pastries and became closely associated with French boulangeries.

🥐 What is a Croissant?

A croissant is a buttery, flaky pastry made from laminated dough. Layers of dough and butter are folded repeatedly before the pastry is shaped and baked until golden and crisp.
